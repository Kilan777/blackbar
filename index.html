Sending a pay stub to a landlord? A bank statement to a lender? A screenshot to the internet? Blackbar finds your SSN, account numbers and other details and permanently removes them, right in your browser.

1. Open your file

Bank statements, pay stubs, tax forms, IDs, screenshots. Scans and photos work too.

2. Check what's hidden

Blackbar marks SSNs, account and routing numbers, card numbers, birth dates and more. Click to unhide, drag to hide anything else.

3. Download

Every page is flattened, so the hidden text is gone, not just covered. You can't copy it or peel it off.

Why not just draw black boxes?

In most PDF editors a black box is a sticker on top of the text. The text is still there, and anyone can copy it out or delete the box. Blackbar rebuilds each page as a clean image with the details burned out, and strips hidden metadata and photo location data.

Is it really private?

Yes. There's no server to upload to. Your file is processed by code running in this tab, and this page's security policy blocks it from sending data anywhere. Close the tab and it's gone.
